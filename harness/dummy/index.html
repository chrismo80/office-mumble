office-mumble · a synthetic colleague

This is not a person. It is a voice the harness makes, so a floor can be listened to without a second human on the other end. Leave this tab open and walk over in the other one.

Voices: ?source=file a recording beside the page, the closest thing to a colleague · ?source=tone a continuous tone · ?source=bursts generated syllables with pauses · ?source=silence a connection and nothing to send.

A recording of yours goes into harness/dummy/public/ — any .wav — and lands beside this page as ./voice.wav, or wherever ?voice= points. The repository carries none of its own: without one this page falls back to generated syllables. Something short is enough, it is played in a loop.
